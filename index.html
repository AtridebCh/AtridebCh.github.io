About

I am a cosmologist studying the first billion years of the Universe, from Cosmic Dawn through the Epoch of Reionization. My work combines physical models of these early epochs with machine learning to get the most out of current and upcoming sky surveys.

I am currently a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellow at the Kapteyn Astronomical Institute, University of Groningen, working on LUPCOS (Learning the Universe in the era of Precision COSmology). The project brings together three threads: extending my analytical reionization model CosmoReionMC with more observational data and more detailed astrophysics, coupling semi-numerical galaxy formation models with modern parameter estimation techniques, and applying recent machine learning methods to cosmological N-body simulations to extract as much information as possible, as a precursor to parameter inference for the next generation of surveys.

I also enjoy taking these methods beyond astronomy. In 2025 I did a three-month data analysis internship on a digital health project with Altoida, looking for early signs of cognitive decline in app-based behavioural data. Since November 2026, I have been working as a data consultant at General Cognitive, and my time is now split equally: 50% at the University of Groningen on my MSCA fellowship and 50% at General Cognitive.

Before Groningen, I was a postdoctoral fellow at IUCAA, Pune, and completed my integrated PhD at the National Centre for Radio Astrophysics (NCRA-TIFR), Pune, under Prof. Tirthankar Roy Choudhury.
